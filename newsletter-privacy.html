Newsletter privacy

When you subscribe to Colorado Now's email newsletter, we send your email address to Brevo, our email delivery provider. Brevo sends a confirmation email and manages your subscription, email delivery, and unsubscribe status.

You join the newsletter after confirming your address. We use the address to send Colorado Now newsletters, and each newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. You do not need an account on our website to subscribe.

Our signup form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to prevent automated abuse. Security checks process network and device information. Our backend also keeps temporary, protected rate-limit identifiers derived from email and network addresses; these identifiers are not publicly accessible and are periodically removed once they are more than two days old.

Brevo processes delivery information such as failed deliveries, unsubscribe requests, and campaign engagement according to the account's tracking settings. Double-opt-in confirmation requests from this website do not opt you into individually identified open and click tracking.

Our newsletter mailing list is not published on the website or stored in public website files. To ask about your subscription or request removal, contact newsletter@thecoloradonow.com.
