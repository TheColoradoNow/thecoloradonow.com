About Us

We are a team of passionate reporters who’ve recognized a gap in community-centered journalism and are working to fill it. Our purpose is to keep Coloradans informed on the events, civic gatherings and issues shaping their communities. We value nuance, nonpartisanship, and truth over profit—to report accurately, regardless of financial pressures. Without trusted local news, polarization and misinformation deepens. Fraud and deception go uninvestigated. And, citizens lose touch with the communities around them. The foundation of Colorado Now is to restore trust in local journalism—to bring back the confidence people once had reading the morning paper, reimagined for the modern age.
